玄仪 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 20 日 · 适用版本 1.0

一句话说明

玄仪 1.0 是一次性付费下载的离线 App。你的问题、决定、结果与出生资料都保存在这台设备的 App 沙盒里；App 不发起任何网络请求，没有账号、没有登录、没有第三方 SDK、没有广告标识，也没有任何形式的按次付费解读。购买由 App Store 完成，玄仪不接触你的支付信息。App 内的插画类素材由生成式 AI 产出（见 App 内图像素材的来源），与你的资料无关。

1. 在本机处理的数据

玄仪会在设备本地处理以下内容：

  • 你主动写下的问题、选择的主题、后续决定与结果；
  • 你主动填写的出生资料，包括出生日期和时间、性别、地点名称、经纬度与时区；
  • 完成入门引导等不含正文的本机设置。

出生资料与私人动态是两份独立存储，删除其中一份不会连带删除另一份。

四式排盘全部在本机完成，规则库随包分发。1.0 不提供深度解读，因此不产生解读语境，也不产生逐次同意审计。

2. 数据如何使用

  • 私人动态用于在本机显示问题、决定、回看与结果轨迹；
  • 出生资料用于在本机生成需要本命信息的盘（八字与西方占星）；缺失或无法读取时，这两式如实受阻，不用默认资料补盘；
  • 本机设置用于恢复你明确选择的界面与安全状态。

这些内容不用于广告、跨 App 追踪、用户画像、模型训练或人工审阅——1.0 没有任何把它们送出设备的通道。

3. 数据存放与保护

私人动态与出生资料分别保存在 iOS Application Support 目录下的玄仪 App 沙盒中。它们采用 iOS 完整文件保护、受保护暂存与原子替换；相关目录或文件会请求并回读系统备份排除状态。私人动态保存会回读暂存与最终文件的完整保护状态，并从最终文件重新解码、逐值核对；每次保存或整体删除也会清理由该 store 严格命名的崩溃暂存文件并再次枚举确认。App 进入非前台状态时，会用隐私遮罩覆盖主页面与敏感弹层快照。

这些措施不等于独立 App 锁：设备已经解锁、且能打开玄仪的人仍可能看到内容。备份排除是 App 向系统声明的存储要求，并非对 Apple 系统服务的绝对保证；玄仪不读取、管理或承诺删除 Apple 管理的系统备份副本。部分不含正文的本机设置可能由系统备份。

旧版本曾允许用户在 App 中配置模型凭据。1.0 不提供保存或读取入口，并在每次启动时按固定 Keychain 身份尝试删除旧凭据，再独立回读确认不存在。这一步不产生任何网络请求，写入的状态与诊断日志都不包含凭据值。

4. 网络、付费与第三方

当前状态

1.0 没有任何网络请求：没有账号、登录、同步、广告、分析或崩溃上报，也没有第三方 SDK 或远程依赖。系统键盘、系统备份和其他 iOS 能力由 Apple 或你选择的第三方分别处理，并受其政策与设备设置约束。

付费

玄仪 1.0 是一次性付费下载，没有内购、没有订阅、没有广告，也没有按次收费的解读。购买、退款与收据由 App Store 处理；玄仪不接收、不保存你的支付方式、账单地址或 Apple 账号信息。

用户主动导出与系统分享

“我”→“本机记录”提供用户主动触发的文本导出：

  • 默认导出问题、主题、提问时间、决定、结果正文、结果评价和结果时间；
  • 出生资料默认不导出。只有你在导出确认页明确打开“包含出生信息（敏感）”，文件才逐项加入出生日期、出生时间、性别、出生地点、纬度、经度、时区与八字真太阳时偏好；确认页与实际文件共用同一字段清单。

你点击“确认并导出”后，玄仪以原子写入和 iOS 完整文件保护在本机临时目录生成文本文件；只有逐字节回读内容且回读最终文件保护属性为完整保护后，才把它交给 iOS 系统分享面板，此时系统分享服务可读取该文件。写入或任一回读失败时会先删除并确认不存在；若删除也无法确认，App 会保留该临时文件的身份供下次重试，不会丢失清理目标。当你选择目标 App 或服务后，文件可能离开玄仪 App 沙盒；若该目标会联网或传往其他设备，文件也可能离开这台设备。分享面板结束后玄仪删除临时文件并回读确认文件已不存在。若删除或回读失败，App 会明确提示，且在确认旧文件删除前不会创建下一份导出。文件一旦交付，其保存、上传、转发、保留和删除由你的选择以及目标 App 或服务的隐私政策与功能决定，玄仪不能代其控制。

5. 保留与分别删除

本机资料会保留到你删除或卸载 App，没有自动过期清理：

  • “我”→“本机记录”可清除问题、决定、结果及该记录 store 严格命名的崩溃暂存文件；只有重新枚举不存在且重新加载为空后才显示清除完成；
  • “我”→“本命资料”可单独删除出生资料。

删除出生资料不会连带删除私人动态，反之亦然。删除失败或独立回读未确认时，App 不会显示为已删除。

Apple 管理的系统备份不属于玄仪当前可直接查询或删除的范围，可通过 Apple 提供的设备与备份设置管理。

6. 你的选择

你可以不填写或删除出生资料；相应本命方法会如实受阻，不会用默认资料补一张与你无关的盘。你可以跳过或重看入门引导；跳过不会生成个人问题。1.0 没有必须授予的系统权限，也没有需要你授权的网络功能。

7. 政策变更

若数据类型、用途、接收方或产品能力发生实质变化，我们会在新版 App 和公开政策页中更新说明；在法律或平台规则要求时，会重新取得必要同意。

Xuanyi Privacy Policy

Effective 20 September 2026 · Applies to version 1.0 · 中文版本见本页上半部分

In one sentence

Xuanyi 1.0 is a one-time paid download that runs offline. Your questions, decisions, outcomes and birth details are stored in the app's sandbox on this device. The app makes no network requests of any kind. There is no account, no sign-in, no third-party SDK, no advertising identifier, and no pay-per-reading of any form. Purchase is handled by the App Store; Xuanyi never sees your payment information. The illustrations inside the app were produced by generative AI (see Where the in-app imagery comes from) and have nothing to do with your data.

1. What is processed on this device

  • the questions you write, the topic you pick, and the decisions and outcomes you record afterwards;
  • the birth details you choose to enter: birth date and time, sex, place name, latitude, longitude and time zone;
  • local settings that contain no content of their own, such as whether you finished onboarding.

Birth details and your private thread are two separate stores. Deleting one does not delete the other.

All four charts are computed on device, from rule tables bundled with the app. Version 1.0 offers no deep reading, so there is no interpretation context and no per-use consent audit.

2. How it is used

  • Your private thread is used to show, on this device, your question, your decision, your check-back and the outcome trail.
  • Birth details are used on this device to build the charts that require natal information (Bazi and Western astrology). When they are missing or unreadable, those two instruments are blocked honestly — no default data is substituted.
  • Local settings restore the interface and safety state you explicitly chose.

None of this is used for advertising, cross-app tracking, profiling, model training or human review. Version 1.0 has no channel of any kind for sending it off the device.

3. Storage and protection

Your private thread and your birth details are stored separately in the Xuanyi sandbox under the iOS Application Support directory. Both use iOS complete file protection, protected staging files and atomic replacement; the relevant directories and files request backup-exclusion from the system and then read that state back. Saving a thread reads back the protection state of both the staging file and the final file, then re-decodes the final file and checks it value by value. Every save or full deletion also cleans up crash-staging files under that store's strict naming scheme and re-enumerates to confirm. When the app leaves the foreground, a privacy overlay covers snapshots of the main screen and of sensitive sheets.

These measures are not a separate app lock: anyone who can unlock the device and open Xuanyi can still see the content. Backup exclusion is a storage request the app makes to the system, not an absolute guarantee about Apple's system services; Xuanyi does not read, manage or promise to delete backup copies held by Apple. Some local settings that contain no content may be included in system backups.

An older version allowed model credentials to be configured inside the app. Version 1.0 offers no way to save or read them, and on every launch it attempts to delete any old credential under a fixed Keychain identity and then independently reads back to confirm it is gone. That step makes no network request, and neither the stored state nor the diagnostic log contains any credential value.

4. Network, payment and third parties

Current state

Version 1.0 makes no network requests: no account, sign-in, sync, advertising, analytics or crash reporting, and no third-party SDK or remote dependency. The system keyboard, system backup and other iOS capabilities are handled by Apple or by third parties you have chosen, and are governed by their own policies and your device settings.

Payment

Xuanyi 1.0 is a one-time paid download with no in-app purchases, no subscription, no advertising and no pay-per-reading. Purchases, refunds and receipts are handled by the App Store; Xuanyi does not receive or store your payment method, billing address or Apple Account information.

Exports you start yourself

Profile → On-device records offers a text export that only you can trigger:

  • by default it exports the question, topic, time asked, decision, outcome text, outcome rating and outcome time;
  • birth details are not exported by default. Only if you explicitly turn on “Include birth information (sensitive)” on the confirmation screen does the file add birth date, birth time, sex, birthplace, latitude, longitude, time zone and the true-solar-time preference. The confirmation screen and the actual file share one field list.

After you tap Confirm and export, Xuanyi writes the text file atomically into a local temporary directory with iOS complete file protection. Only after reading the content back byte for byte, and confirming the final file's protection attribute is complete protection, does it hand the file to the iOS share sheet — at which point the system share service can read it. If the write or either read-back fails, the file is deleted and the deletion confirmed; if deletion cannot be confirmed, the app keeps that temporary file's identity for the next attempt rather than losing track of it. Once you pick a target app or service, the file may leave the Xuanyi sandbox, and if that target uses the network or syncs to other devices, the file may leave this device too. When the share sheet closes, Xuanyi deletes the temporary file and reads back to confirm it is gone; if deletion or read-back fails it says so plainly, and it will not create the next export until the old file is confirmed deleted. Once delivered, how the file is stored, uploaded, forwarded, retained or deleted is decided by your choice and by the target app or service's own policy — Xuanyi cannot control that on your behalf.

5. Retention and separate deletion

On-device data is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app; there is no automatic expiry:

  • Profile → On-device records clears questions, decisions, outcomes and the crash-staging files under that store's strict naming scheme; it reports success only after re-enumeration finds nothing and a reload comes back empty;
  • Profile → Birth information deletes the birth details on their own.

Deleting birth details does not delete your private thread, and vice versa. If a deletion fails, or the independent read-back does not confirm it, the app does not report it as deleted.

System backups managed by Apple are outside what Xuanyi can query or delete directly; manage them through Apple's device and backup settings.

6. Your choices

You may leave birth details blank or delete them; the natal methods will then be blocked honestly rather than standing in someone else's chart. You may skip or re-watch onboarding; skipping generates no personal question. Version 1.0 requires no system permission and has no network feature for you to authorise.

7. Changes to this policy

If the categories of data, their purposes, their recipients or the product's capabilities change materially, we will update this policy in a new version of the app and on this public page, and will obtain any consent required by law or platform rules again.
